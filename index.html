Yard Regulations

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO.

Yard Rules

  • Speed limit: 5 mph at all times
  • Do not park on scales
  • No passengers allowed on truck beds or trailers
  • All passengers must exit the vehicle and stand in the designated area while being unloaded by mechanical equipment
  • Shirt and shoes must be worn at all times
  • Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult at all times
  • Valid state-issued photo ID required to be on file
  • Items must be sorted before going to small scales
  • Non-ferrous loads must be pre-sorted by customer before bringing in to scrap
  • Deductions will be made for dirt, tires, wood, concrete, etc. at the discretion of the yard supervisor
  • $100 minimum charge for sorting items with radiation (subject to rejection)
  • Wes' Recycling is not responsible for damage to vehicles, tires, or trailers

Vehicle Requirements

  • Title required on all complete vehicles
  • Vehicles 35 years or older can be brought in without title — a Bill of Sale will be filled out
  • All fluids must be drained
  • Mercury switches removed
  • Air conditioning units must be disconnected
  • Refrigerants must be removed from all vehicles and appliances
  • Up to 4 passenger tires per vehicle before deducting tire weight
  • Will NOT accept hybrid or electric vehicles unless ALL EV batteries have been removed

Tank Rules

  • Tanks larger than 100 gallons must have a 3' × 3' hole
  • Fuel tanks must have an 8" × 8" hole and be dry
  • Propane tanks, helium tanks, and fire extinguishers must have valve removed OR a hole big enough to see into the container
  • All tanks must be empty and dry

We Will NOT Accept

  • Beer Kegs
  • Paint
  • Items containing Refrigerant
  • Radioactive Materials
  • Explosive Materials
  • Capacitors or anything with PCBs
  • Neon Signs
  • Thermostats
  • Mercury Switches
  • Closed Propane Tanks
  • Hybrid/Electric Vehicles (unless batteries removed)
  • Freon
Refusal to follow regulations may result in refusal to do business with you.
